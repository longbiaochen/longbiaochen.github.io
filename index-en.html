Longbiao Chen is an Associate Professor and Doctoral Supervisor in the Department of Computer Science, School of Informatics, Xiamen University. He is affiliated with the Fujian Key Laboratory of Sensing and Computing for Smart City and the Key Laboratory of Multimedia Trusted Perception and Efficient Computing, Ministry of Education. He has been recognized by national, Fujian provincial, Xiamen municipal, and Xiamen University talent programs.

He received his Ph.D. from Zhejiang University in 2016 under the supervision of Gang Pan, and a second Ph.D. from Sorbonne University in 2018 under the supervision of Daqing Zhang. Before returning to China, he served as an assistant researcher at Institut Mines-Telecom, France.

His research focuses on crowd intelligence, embodied intelligence, spatial computing, ubiquitous computing, and domain-specific large language models. He has published 87 papers, including 14 CCF-A papers and 17 papers in CCF-B venues or Clarivate JCR-ranked journals as first or corresponding author. His work received the 2022 ACM SIGSPATIAL China Rising Star Award and ACM UbiComp Honorable Mention Awards in both 2015 and 2016. He holds 20 granted invention patents and has led six competitive research projects, including NSFC General and Young Scientists Fund projects, a national defense basic research project, and a national high-resolution Earth observation project.

He contributes to the research community through leadership and service in ACM SIGSPATIAL China, the CCF Ubiquitous Computing Committee, CCF YOCSEF Xiamen, journal editorial boards, and international conference program committees. He teaches undergraduate and graduate courses in software engineering and pattern recognition, organizes student research and competition programs, and mentors award-winning innovation and entrepreneurship teams.
